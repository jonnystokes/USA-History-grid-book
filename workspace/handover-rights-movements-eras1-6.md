## Legal Status Under Coverture

The doctrine of coverture affirmed that a woman, once married, was totally subsumed, or "covered," under her husband's person. A married woman, or feme covert, had no legal status; did not control any property, even if she brought it to the marriage; and ceded to her husband full rights to all incomes and wages she earned.
