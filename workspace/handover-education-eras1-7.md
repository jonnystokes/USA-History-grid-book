**The triad.** Three items, three clauses, or three sentences in a row, because three sounds finished.
> **Weak:** "They came for land, for freedom, and for a chance to begin again."
> **Strong:** "They came for land. Most of them were farmers who could not get land at home."

> **Weak:** "They built the roads. They dug the canals. They laid the rails."
