## Before 1500
<!-- hb-zoom level="era" -->
Justice among Native nations generally aimed at repair rather than punishment: making the injured family whole.
<!-- /hb-zoom -->
<!-- hb-zoom level="span" label="Restitution and banishment" -->
Restitution; clan responsibility for a member's acts; councils hearing disputes; banishment as a serious penalty.

## 1800 to 1850
<!-- hb-zoom level="era" -->
Cities create the first full-time police forces, and states build competing kinds of prisons.
<!-- /hb-zoom -->
<!-- hb-zoom level="span" label="The first police and the prison systems" -->
The first modern police departments (Boston 1838, New York 1845 [VERIFY both]); vigilance committees taking the law themselves; the Auburn and Pennsylvania prison systems argued over on two continents.

<!-- hb-zoom level="era" -->
