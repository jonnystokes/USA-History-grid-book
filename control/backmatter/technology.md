## Questions

- Q: Why did planters enslave far more people after the cotton gin? A: One gin cleaned about 50 pounds of cotton a day, work that took several hundred hours by hand. Planters grew far more cotton and enslaved far more people to grow and pick it, nearly 4 million by 1860.
- Q: Why could an enslaved inventor not get a patent? A: Only the inventor could get a patent, and only by swearing an oath. In 1858 Attorney General Jeremiah Black ruled that an enslaved man was not a citizen and could not swear it, and that the enslaver could not take the patent instead.
- Q: What went wrong when people trusted a computer's answer? A: In 2023 two lawyers filed fake court decisions made by ChatGPT and were fined $5,000. In 2020 Detroit police held Robert Williams for 30 hours after a face-recognition search matched him by mistake.
