## Questions

- Q: Why did colonists who crossed the ocean to worship freely punish other people for their faith? A: The Puritans wanted freedom for their own way of worship, and they ran Massachusetts by it. Their leaders banished Roger Williams and Anne Hutchinson and hanged four Quakers in Boston.
- Q: How did Americans stop paying taxes to support churches? A: Baptists and James Madison defeated a new church tax in Virginia, where Jefferson's religious freedom law passed in 1786. Massachusetts ended the last state church tax in 1833.
- Q: How long did US officials treat Native ceremonies as crimes? A: For ninety-five years, from Hiram Price's rules of 1883 until members of Congress passed the American Indian Religious Freedom Act in 1978.
