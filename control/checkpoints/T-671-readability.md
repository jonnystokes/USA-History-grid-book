## SUBJECT NOTES (the plan, written before any edit)

**Spine:** Americans stopped whipping and branding people in public and started locking them in
prisons, and in every era whether a person got a fair trial depended on who that person was.

**Three questions** (word for word in the closing):
1. How did punishment move from the whipping post to the prison cell?
2. Who kept order before there were police?
3. What happened to the people in mobs who killed without a trial?

**Anchor:** Clarence Earl Gideon, Raiford prison, Florida, 1961 to 1963: refused a lawyer because
Florida gave free lawyers only to people facing death, wrote to the US Supreme Court in pencil, won
on March 18, 1963, found not guilty at his second trial after an hour (all in era 9's story).
