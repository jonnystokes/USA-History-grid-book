## SUBJECT NOTES (decided before writing)

**Spine:** Americans cleared this land, used it up and then saved pieces of it, and at almost every
step the people who lost most had the least say: first Native nations, later poor and Black
neighborhoods.

**Three questions (word for word in the closing):**
1. How did Native nations shape the land before Europeans came?
2. What happened to the 30 million bison and the billions of passenger pigeons?
3. Who was pushed out when parks were made, and who lived next to the waste?

**Anchor:** Miantonomi's 1642 speech to the Montaukett on Long Island (scythes, axes, hogs on the
clam banks, "we shall all be starved"), written down by Lion Gardener; English officials sentenced
him to death the next year. Already in era 3.

**Five minutes?** "Yosemite and the Ahwahneechee" in 1850 to 1900 (about 850 words: a park made by
burning a people's food and driving them out).

**Can skip:** "Whaling from Nantucket", "The forest reserves", "Plastic in the rain".

**Reading time:** words / 200 rounded to 5 (before: 17,891 words = 90 minutes; recompute at end).

Mayer story. Others under 4,000; add one only if an era grows past it.
