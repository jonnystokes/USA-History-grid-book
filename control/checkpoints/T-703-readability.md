**Anchor:** Buffalo Bird Woman's told life, published in 1921: her mothers' earth lodge on the Missouri, floor more than forty feet across, fire in the center, owned by the women; four wives of her father, all sisters, and she was taught to call all four her mothers; by 1921 "We no longer live in an earth lodge, but in a house with chimneys," and her son's wife cooked by a stove. 1. Who lived together under one roof, from the longhouse to today?
