**Era through-lines (first sentence after the strip, pilot fix 2):**
1 Native nations made music for every part of life and taught songs instead of writing them.
2 The first European music on record here is church singing and soldiers' trumpets, with Native people watching.
3 Colonists brought their church singing, and Spanish officials punished Pueblo people for their own.
4 Two fights over music: New England churches over singing from notes, South Carolina lawmakers over drums.
5 Americans began writing and printing their own music, and Black musicians appear mostly in runaway notices.
6 Enslaved people made songs with their own meanings, and white men in blackface copied Black music and kept the money.
7 After the war, Black singers carried the spirituals around the world, while a school officer worked to silence Native children's drums.
8 Record companies sold blues, jazz and country to millions, and most of the money went to the companies and copyright holders.
9 Black musicians made rock and roll, soul and hip-hop, and others often made more from the songs than they did.
10 Most Americans now stream music, and a stream pays the owner of the recording a fraction of a cent.
