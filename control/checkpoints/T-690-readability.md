**Spine:** American cities were built by people who decided where the streets, pipes and buildings would go, and who could live where.

**Three questions:**
1. Why do so many American cities have straight streets that cross in a grid? (Laws of the Indies 1573, Penn and Holme 1682, the 1811 Manhattan grid)
2. How did people get clean water into crowded cities? (Centre Square 1801, Croton 1842, Chicago's river reversed 1900, Hetch Hetchy)
