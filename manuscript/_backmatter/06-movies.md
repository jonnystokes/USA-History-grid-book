# Movies

Every movie and TV show listed in the story boxes, from A to Z, with what each box says about it. A title that begins with "The" or "A" is sorted by its next word, so *The Revenant* is under R. Some of these were made for adults, so check each one's rating and ask a grown-up before you watch it.

- **Amazing Grace (2018)**: a documentary film of Aretha Franklin's 1972 gospel concert in a Los Angeles church (Chapter 33)
